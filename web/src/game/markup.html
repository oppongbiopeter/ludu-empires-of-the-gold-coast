How to Play

Get all 4 tokens from your yard onto the Golden Stool. You need an exact count to sit the last step.

Leaving home. A piece stays in the yard until you roll a 6. That 6 walks six tiles from your colored gate. Once the piece is on the path, any number moves it that many spaces.

One die is the usual game. A six earns another roll. Options can switch to two dice. Choose a piece, or let the dice move for you.

Cards. Before the race, choose 5 cards from your clan’s deck. Each clan has its own power-ups. Play one card a turn, then it is discarded. A priestess can bring a discarded card back. If your hand is empty, you still roll and move.

Story is a separate road of Invaders. Instant play is still the four Gold Coast empires: you against 3, online against 1–3, or 2–4 on one phone.

Priestess Revive

She returns one discarded tactic to your hand. You can play it on a later turn. She does not return herself — after this she is spent. She also blesses one piece still in your yard (+2 Strength this turn).
