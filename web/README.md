# LUDU: Empires of the Gold Coast

A dice-and-race board game in the spirit of West African ludo, set on the Gold Coast. Four empires — Ashanti, Dagbon, Fante, and Ewe — race four pieces each from their yards to the Golden Stool. You can play it in the browser with no account.

## How a match works

- Roll the die on the board. The number you see is the number of tiles that piece visits. It steps from square to square and does not skip.
- A piece leaves its yard only on a **6**. That 6 still walks six tiles, starting at that empire’s gate. Once a piece is on the path, any roll moves it that many spaces.
- Only the empire whose turn it is can move, and only pieces of that color.
- An exact count is required to sit the last step on the Stool.
- Before the race, each player chooses **5 cards** from their clan’s deck. On your turn, tap **Play** on one of them — before or after you roll — or play none. The card is discarded. You can see the discard under your hand.
- **Priestess revive.** She returns one discarded tactic of your choice to your hand, to be played on a later turn. She does not return herself; she is spent. If the discard is empty, she only blesses a piece still in the yard (+2 Strength that turn). If you open her by mistake, send her back. An empty hand never ends the race.
- When your number is spent, or nothing can legally move, press **End Turn**. A six (one die) or doubles (two dice) does not pass the turn — you roll again. The button says so.
- Land on a power-up and it fires. Some help you, some hinder the other empires, and some change the whole board. Each power-up has its own sound and stays on screen long enough to read. A new one then appears.
- Land on a rival with equal or higher Strength to send them home, unless they stand on a safe star.
- Each leader may be called once. Their portrait is on the empire you pick, and again on that empire’s home.

## Ways to play

- **Vs AI.** You against three rival empires. Difficulty: Easy, Normal, Hard, or Advanced.
- **Story.** A separate road, in the manner of a level map. You march through Invaders — challengers from Benin, Nigeria, Côte d’Ivoire, and Togo — and a win opens the next gate. Instant play is not this mode.
- **Solo — Ancestors.** One empire against a timed ancestral deck. Three catastrophes end the race.
- **One phone.** Two, three, or four players, pass the device. Each human brings their own five cards.
- **Online.** A simulated table against one, two, or three opponents. You still choose the five cards you bring. No account required to sit down.
- Guest play is the default. A local login is optional if you want a name on the records, achievements, and the cloud vault kept in this browser.

## Options

Open **Options** from the home screen or during a match.

- **Choose piece** or **Move for me.**
- **Gameplay speed** — Slow, Normal, Fast, or the slider.
- **Power-up reveal** — a separate slider. Lower is slower.
- **One die or two.** One die is the usual game. A six earns another roll. Two dice are optional; doubles earn another roll.
- **Safe houses.** On by default.
- **Barriers.** Off by default. Two of your pieces on the same tile can block rivals.
- **Need a 6 to leave the yard.** On by default. Turn it off only if you want any number to leave home. When it is on, that six still walks six tiles.
- **Capturing piece also returns.** Off by default.
- **Extra roll after a capture.** Off by default.
- **Turn the board** in quarter-turns if a seat is hard to read.
- **Language:** English, French, Italian, Indonesian, German, Portuguese, and Spanish.

## Invaders

Story challengers. They take a colored yard on the same board. They are not a fifth seat in instant play. Their captains are fictional. **History & Leaders** on the home screen separates those captains from the stools and kingdoms they are drawn from: Asante and the Golden Stool, Dagbon, the Fante coast, the Ga stool of Accra, Anlo and the road from Notsie, Dahomey, the Hausa cities, Ile-Ife and Oyo, the Baoulé crossing, and the Kabyè highlands.

## What this edition does not claim

Same-room play is pass-and-play on one screen. There is no Bluetooth table. The online table in this build is simulated. Store listings, other studios, and other ludo titles are not this game.
