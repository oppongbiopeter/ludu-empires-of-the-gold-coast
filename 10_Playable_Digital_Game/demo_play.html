How to Play

Classic Ludu race on a four-empire board. Get all 4 tokens from your colored yard to the Golden Stool.

Roll → select token → advance. Capture weaker/equal foes. Land on power tiles. Play hand cards (Gwent-style). Leader once per game. Doubles = extra turn.
